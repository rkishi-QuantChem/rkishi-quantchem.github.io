RESEARCH

Research

Our research explores the electronic structure of molecules, interactions between molecules, and the evolution of quantum states, drawing on theoretical and computational chemistry.

We seek to understand molecular properties and phenomena in terms of the quantum mechanical principles that govern them, and to use this understanding to guide the design of molecules with new properties and functions.

Our research spans a broad range of systems and methods. We develop theories and models, investigate molecular systems through computation, and draw on experimental findings to deepen our understanding and identify new scientific questions.

Research Cases

  1. RC-01 — Open-Shell Electronic Structure & Molecular Properties

    We investigate how electron pairing and interactions between electrons shape molecular electronic structures and influence properties such as optical and magnetic responses.

  2. RC-02 — Molecular Interaction & Collective Structure

    We seek to understand how interactions between molecules give rise to changes in electronic structure, chemical bonding, and molecular properties, and how these effects can guide the design of molecular assemblies.

  3. RC-03 — Quantum Dynamics & Molecular–Environment Design

    We investigate how molecular electronic and spin states evolve over time, and explore how molecular structures and surrounding environments may be used to control quantum-state conversion and molecular functions.
